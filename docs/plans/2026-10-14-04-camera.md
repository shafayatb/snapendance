## Decisions

| # | Decision | Choice |
|---|----------|--------|
| C1 | Permission flow | `permission_handler`: status, request, permanent denial, and app settings (`C8`). Checked again on every resume. |
| C2 | Orientation | The Flutter app is portrait-only (attendance already is), so preview, focus mapping, and controls have one layout. |
| C3 | Preview | First back camera, `ResolutionPreset.veryHigh`, no audio. Full-bleed, cover-cropped (no stretching). |
| C4 | Zoom buttons and lenses | One zoom value, clamped to the camera's min/max, shared by pinch (relative to the zoom at gesture start), slider, and buttons. Buttons: the minimum when below 1.0 (ultra-wide through CameraX's logical camera), 1, 2, 5, 10, each only when within range; labels come from the real values. Switching between several physical back cameras is not implemented (limitation; `availableCameras()` is recorded on the phone). |
| C5 | Flash | Off → Auto → On cycle. Offered only when Android reports a flash unit (`FEATURE_CAMERA_FLASH` over the existing platform channel; the plugin cannot tell). Otherwise the icon is dimmed and a tap shows "Flash isn't available on this camera" (`C5`). |
| C6 | Flip button | Omitted: the brief requires back cameras only. Design deviation. |
| C7 | Focus | Tap maps through the cover crop to the full preview's normalized point (CameraX uses a display-oriented metering factory); sets focus, and exposure when supported. Indicator: 80 dp amber ring with dot, label "FOCUS", scale 1.2 → 1.0 in 150 ms, hold 1.5 s, fade 200 ms. CameraX returns to continuous focus by itself. Unsupported focus shows "Tap to focus isn't supported on this camera" and no ring. |
| C8 | Lifecycle | Release the camera when the app goes inactive or to the background, or the screen closes; reopen on resume. Late results from an open that was overtaken are closed. |
